Privacy Policy

To-Do Task Manager · Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how To-Do Task Manager handles information when you use the application. It also explains the use of advertisements and rewarded advertising features provided through third-party advertising services.

1. Information Stored by the App

The app may store information that you enter or select, including task names, due dates, priorities, completion status, completed-task history, theme preferences and your Privacy Policy consent preference.

Task-related information is stored locally on your device using the app's local storage features. The app does not require you to create an account to use its basic task management features.

The app also stores access-related settings on your device, such as the expiry time of an activated 12-hour access period.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Locally stored information is used to manage your tasks, display progress, show completed-task history, remember your selected theme and remember your Privacy Policy consent choice.

Access-related settings are used to manage the app's advertising-based access options.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

This app uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements. The app may display banner advertisements and rewarded video advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process certain information in connection with advertising. Depending on the applicable settings, region and services, this may include device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, diagnostic information and information used for advertising measurement or personalization.

Such information is handled under Google's applicable privacy policies and advertising settings. The developer does not control Google's independent data practices.

For more information, please review: Google Privacy Policy.

4. Rewarded Ads and App Access

The app offers optional rewarded advertisements as one method of accessing the workspace.

  • 12-hour access: Completing two rewarded advertisements activates access for 12 hours.
  • Session access: Completing one rewarded advertisement provides access for the current app session only.

Rewarded advertisements must be completed successfully for the corresponding access option to be activated. Access settings are stored on the device.

Activating 12-hour access does not necessarily remove banner advertisements. Banner advertisements may continue to appear while using the app.

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising. Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

The developer does not operate a cloud account system or task synchronization service in the current version. If additional services such as analytics, cloud backup or crash reporting are introduced, this policy will be reviewed and updated as necessary.

6. Storage and Deletion

Task information stored locally may remain on your device between app sessions.

Locally stored information and app preferences may be deleted by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application. Some advertising-related information may be handled separately by Google and may be subject to Google's own retention and deletion practices.

7. Sensitive Information

Please avoid entering passwords, financial details, government identification numbers or other sensitive personal information into task names or descriptions.

8. Children's Privacy

To-Do Task Manager is a general-purpose task management application. It is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

If the app's intended audience or data practices change, appropriate privacy protections and applicable legal requirements will be reviewed.

9. Security

The developer takes reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features, advertising services or data practices change. The latest version will be made available through the app's Privacy Policy page.
