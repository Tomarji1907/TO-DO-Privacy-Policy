Privacy Policy

To-Do Task Manager · Last updated: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the To-Do Task Manager app handles information when you use it.

1. Information Stored by the App

The current app stores task names, due dates, priorities, completion status, completed-task history, your selected theme, and your Privacy Policy consent preference in local storage on your device. The app does not require an account.

2. How Your Information Is Used

This information is used to manage tasks, display progress, show completed-task history, remember your theme, and remember your consent choice. The current front-end code does not itself send this information to a developer-operated server.

3. Storage and Deletion

Locally stored information may remain on your device between sessions. It may be removed when you clear the app data or relevant browser storage, or uninstall the app. There is currently no in-app cloud backup or cross-device sync.

4. Third-Party Services

The current front-end code does not itself include advertising or analytics code. If advertising, analytics, crash reporting, cloud sync, or other third-party services are added, their data practices must be reviewed and this policy updated accordingly.

5. Sensitive Information

Avoid entering passwords, financial details, identification numbers, or other sensitive information into task names.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is a general-purpose task manager. If its intended audience or data practices change, its privacy protections should be reviewed before release.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. If a material change requires renewed consent, the app should request acceptance of the updated policy version.
