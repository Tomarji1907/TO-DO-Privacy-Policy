Privacy Policy

To-Do Task Manager

Effective Date: 24 October 2026
Last Updated: 24 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how To-Do Task Manager handles information when you use the app. The app helps you organize tasks, set due dates and priorities, monitor your progress, review completed tasks, and use a focus timer.

1. Information Stored by the App

The current version stores certain information locally in your browser or app storage on your device. This may include:

  • Task names and due dates you enter.
  • Task priorities and completion status.
  • Completed-task history.
  • Your selected light or dark theme.
  • Your saved Privacy Policy consent preference.

The app does not require you to create an account to use these features. The tasks and preferences described above are stored locally by the current front-end code.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Locally stored information is used to display and manage your tasks, calculate your progress, show your completed-task history, remember your theme preference, and remember your Privacy Policy consent.

The app displays the date and time using your device's clock. Its focus timer counts down while the app's code is running. The motivational messages on the splash screen are built into the app.

The current front-end code does not itself provide a cloud account or cross-device synchronization. It does not itself send task names or task lists to a developer-operated server.

3. Privacy Policy Consent

Before entering the task manager, the app asks you to open the Privacy Policy and tick the agreement checkbox. You must then select the acceptance button to continue.

Your consent preference is saved locally on your device so the app can remember your choice the next time you open it. The current app code stores a consent-version value rather than sending your acceptance to a remote server.

If the Privacy Policy is materially changed, the app may require you to review and accept the updated version. Clearing app or browser storage may also remove the saved consent preference.

4. Storage, Retention and Deletion

Your tasks, completed-task history, theme preference and consent preference may remain stored on your device between app sessions. The information is generally retained until you delete it, clear the relevant storage, or remove the app and its associated data.

You can delete individual tasks using the available app controls. You can also clear completed-task history from within the app. Clearing all app or browser storage may remove additional saved tasks and preferences.

The app does not currently provide an online account for recovering deleted data. If a task is important, keep your own separate copy.

5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

The current front-end code does not itself include advertising code, analytics code, cloud synchronization, or an online task submission service.

If the app is packaged using a third-party app wrapper, the wrapper or distribution platform may have separate technical practices. Any future addition of advertising, analytics, crash reporting, online storage or other third-party services must be reviewed and this policy updated as necessary.

Third-party services, if added in a future version, may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Device Permissions and Sensitive Information

The current task manager features described in this policy do not require you to provide your real name, phone number, precise location, contacts or calendar information.

Please avoid entering passwords, payment card details, financial information, private identification numbers, or other sensitive information into task names or notes.

7. Focus Timer and Notifications

The focus timer is an in-app countdown. It is not a guaranteed system alarm. Its behavior may be affected if the app is closed, suspended, or restricted by your browser or operating system.

Do not rely on the timer for emergency alerts or other time-critical notifications.

8. Children's Privacy

To-Do Task Manager is a general-purpose task organizer. The current front-end code does not require children or other users to submit personal information to a developer-operated server.

If the app's audience, online services or information practices change, its privacy protections and this section should be reviewed before the updated version is released.

9. Data Security

Information stored locally may be accessible to someone who has access to your unlocked device or its app data. Protect your device with appropriate security settings.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. The current app does not provide a separate online backup of your tasks.

10. Your Choices

  • Choose what information you enter into tasks.
  • Add, complete or delete tasks using the app controls.
  • Clear completed-task history from the app.
  • Choose between the available light and dark themes.
  • Decide whether to accept the Privacy Policy and continue.
  • Clear app or browser storage to remove locally stored data.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features, data practices or applicable requirements change. The latest version should be published at the official Privacy Policy webpage used by the app.

If a change requires renewed consent, the app should be updated to use a new Privacy Policy version so that users can review it again before continuing.
