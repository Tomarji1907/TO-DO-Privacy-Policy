Privacy Policy

TO-DO App — Smart Daily Organizer

Effective Date: 23 October 2026

Last Updated: 23 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TO-DO App handles information when you use the application. The app helps users organise daily tasks, track progress, use a countdown timer, set an in-app alarm and optionally save a star rating.

1. Tasks and Local Storage

Tasks you create, their completion status and related task information are stored in the browser or app storage on your device using local storage.

The current version is not designed to upload task lists to a cloud account or synchronise them across devices. Avoid entering passwords, financial details or other sensitive information into your tasks.

2. Splash Screen and App Preferences

The app displays a splash screen and a Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy consent screen. The app saves a local consent preference so it can remember that you accepted the policies.

3. Timer, Clock and Alarm

The app displays the time using your device's clock and provides timer and alarm features. These features operate within the app while it is running and may depend on browser, device, sound and background-execution settings.

The current version does not require access to your personal calendar. An alarm is not guaranteed to sound if the app is closed, suspended or restricted by the operating system.

4. Rating Feature

If you submit a star rating, the selected rating and your rating preference are saved locally on your device. The app uses this preference to avoid showing the rating prompt again after you submit a rating.

Submitting a rating in the current version does not automatically send it to the developer. Clearing app storage or reinstalling the app may reset this preference.

5. Support and Email

If you tap the support email link, your device may open an installed email application with a prepared message. You choose what to write and whether to send it.

If you send us an email, we will receive the information included in that message, such as your email address, complaint, question or suggestion. We use it to respond to your request and improve the app.

Please do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive personal information in support emails.

6. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The current standalone code does not intentionally send task content to a third-party server or include a weather service, analytics service or advertising SDK.

If a future version includes advertising, analytics, crash reporting, cloud storage or other third-party services, those services may collect or process information under their own privacy policies. This policy must be updated to describe any such services before that version is released.

7. Data Sharing and Security

The current app's task and rating data is stored locally on your device. Information you independently choose to send through email is delivered using your email provider.

Keep your device secure. No electronic storage or communication method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored tasks, consent preferences and rating preferences remain on the device until they are changed or removed, or the relevant app data is cleared. Uninstalling the app may remove local data, depending on your device and app configuration.

Emails you send to support may remain in your email account and our received messages for as long as needed to respond, maintain relevant records or meet applicable legal obligations.

9. Your Choices

  • Choose what information you enter into tasks.
  • Add, complete or delete tasks using the app controls.
  • Choose whether to submit an optional star rating.
  • Choose whether to send a support email.
  • Clear app data to remove locally stored preferences and tasks.

10. Children's Privacy

TO-DO App is a general-purpose task organiser. The developer does not intend to collect children's personal information through the current standalone app. If future versions introduce online services or data collection, appropriate privacy protections must be considered.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if app features, data practices or legal requirements change. The latest version should be published at the app's official privacy-policy webpage, where available.
